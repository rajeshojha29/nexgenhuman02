How it works

Measure, treat, re-test

Every programme follows the same three steps. The re-test is what tells you whether the plan is working.

  1. Measure

    A baseline visit records where you are today. You leave with numbers, each one explained in plain language.

    DEXA g/cm² · VO2 max ml/kg/min · RMR kcal/day · HRV ms

  2. Treat

    Your doctor builds a plan around those numbers: training zones, nutrition, recovery sessions, and medical therapies where they are indicated.

    Sauna 60–85 °C · plunge 8–12 °C · HBOT 1.3 ATA · EMS 20 min

  3. Re-test

    The same tests are repeated on the same machines, usually at 6 and 12 weeks. The plan changes if the numbers do not.

    Week 0 → week 6 → week 12

Questions

Before you visit

What happens at a Discovery visit?

It takes about 90 minutes. You have a short consultation, an InBody scan, a guided sauna and cold plunge circuit, and a red light session. You leave with your readings and a suggested plan. The ₹2,499 fee is credited in full if you join an annual membership.

How do membership credits work?

Each plan gives you a number of credits every month. A single recovery session uses 1 credit, a contrast circuit 2, an EMS session 3 and a hyperbaric session 4. Up to a quarter of unused credits roll into the next month, and you can buy more at ₹650 each.

Do I need a doctor's referral?

No. Our own doctor screens you before any test or therapy that needs medical clearance, such as VO2 max testing, IV therapy or hyperbaric oxygen.

Is the DEXA scan safe?

DEXA uses a very low dose of X-ray, far lower than a standard chest X-ray. The room is licensed by the Atomic Energy Regulatory Board and the scan is run by a registered technologist. It is not offered during pregnancy.

Who can have IV therapy or hyperbaric oxygen?

Only guests cleared by our doctor on the day. IV drips are prescribed by a physician and given by a registered nurse. Competitive athletes should know that anti-doping rules limit IV infusions to 100 mL per 12 hours, so we do not include larger drips in athlete plans.

Can I pause my membership when I travel?

Annual members can pause for up to 30 days a year with 7 days' notice. Monthly plans can be paused after the first 3 months.

Will these treatments reverse ageing or cure a condition?

No, and we will not promise that. What we offer is accurate measurement, supervised therapies and a plan you can follow. Results differ from person to person.
