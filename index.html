Clinical precision, in a space built for calm.

Hospital-grade testing usually comes with hospital waiting rooms. Here the same equipment sits behind a quiet lounge, and every result is explained to you by the doctor who ordered it.

Measured, not guessed

A DNA-based test for your biological age. DEXA for muscle, fat and bone. A metabolic analyser for VO2 max and resting metabolic rate. ECG, HRV and blood biomarkers to complete the picture.

Doctors decide, technology assists

A physician screens you before any IV, hyperbaric or exercise test. A full-time dermatologist performs every medical skin procedure.

Unhurried by design

Sessions are booked by appointment, so there are no queues. Peak-hour slots are capped to keep the lounge quiet.

How it works

Measure, treat, re-test

Every programme follows the same three steps. The re-test is what tells you whether the plan is working.

  1. Measure

    A baseline visit records where you are today. You leave with numbers, each one explained in plain language.

    Biological age yrs · DEXA g/cm² · VO2 max ml/kg/min · RMR kcal/day · HRV ms

  2. Treat

    Your doctor builds a plan around those numbers: training zones, nutrition, recovery sessions, and medical therapies where they are indicated.

    Sauna 60–85 °C · plunge 8–12 °C · HBOT 1.3 ATA · EMS 20 min

  3. Re-test

    The same tests are repeated on the same machines, usually at 6 and 12 weeks. The plan changes if the numbers do not.

    Week 0 → week 6 → week 12

How recovery credits work

One credit, any recovery session

A contrast circuit, sauna, cold plunge, red light, compression boots, PEMF, cryotherapy or a class each use one credit. Set how often you would come each month and the plan finder shows which plan covers it.

Credits arrive monthly

You receive your recovery credits on the first of each month. A few unused credits roll into the next month: 2 on Recover, 3 on Signature, 4 on Prestige and 6 on Founders' Circle.

Need more?

Extra recovery credits cost ₹650 each, or 10 for ₹6,000, and last 60 days.

Fair booking

Sauna and contrast bookings have a monthly limit on each plan, so the cabin stays available. Cancelling within 4 hours uses the credit.

EMS, massage, hyperbaric, IV drips and facials are not paid for with recovery credits. Signature and above include a set number each month. Beyond that number, and on Recover, they are charged at the single price less your member discount.

Questions
Questions people ask

    

      
What happens at a Discovery visit?
It takes about 90 minutes. You have a short consultation, an InBody scan, a guided sauna and cold plunge circuit, and a red light session. You leave with your readings and a suggested plan. The ₹2,499 fee is credited in full if you join an annual membership.

      
How do recovery credits work?
Every plan includes recovery credits each month. One credit is one recovery session of any kind: a contrast circuit, sauna, cold plunge, red light, compression boots, PEMF, cryotherapy or a class. EMS, massage, hyperbaric, IV drips and facials are included as a set number of sessions on the higher plans. A few unused credits roll into the next month, from 2 to 6 depending on your plan, and extra credits cost ₹650 each.

      
What is the biological-age test?
It is a DNA methylation test on a small blood or cheek-swab sample, run by a partner lab. It estimates how old your body appears compared with your calendar age. Results take about four weeks and your doctor explains them. It is an estimate, not a diagnosis. Every membership includes it, and so do most programmes.

      
Do I need a doctor's referral?
No. Our own doctor screens you before any test or therapy that needs medical clearance, such as VO2 max testing, IV therapy or hyperbaric oxygen.

      
Is the DEXA scan safe?
DEXA uses a very low dose of X-ray, far lower than a standard chest X-ray. The room is licensed by the Atomic Energy Regulatory Board and the scan is run by a registered technologist. It is not offered during pregnancy.

      
Who can have IV therapy or hyperbaric oxygen?
Only guests cleared by our doctor on the day. IV drips are prescribed by a physician and given by a registered nurse. Competitive athletes should know that anti-doping rules limit IV infusions to 100 mL per 12 hours, so we do not include larger drips in athlete plans.

      
Can I pause my membership when I travel?
Annual members can pause for up to 30 days a year with 7 days' notice. Monthly plans can be paused after the first 3 months.

      
Will these treatments reverse ageing or cure a condition?
No, and we will not promise that. What we offer is accurate measurement, supervised therapies and a plan you can follow. Results differ from person to person.
